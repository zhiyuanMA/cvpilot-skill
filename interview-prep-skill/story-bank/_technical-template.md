## Interview-ready version
See ../frameworks/technical-narrative-framework.md for the full structure — use the short form for implementation/debugging stories, the long form for system-design-level decisions.

**Context** — the system/project, its scale, and the constraints in play
**Decision point** — what specifically needed to be solved or decided
**Options considered** — at least two, with real trade-offs (not a strawman)
**Decision & reasoning** — what was chosen and why, in your own judgment
**Outcome** — result, with numbers where possible
**Reflection** — what you'd do differently, or what you learned
