# Company Profiles — Same Story, Different Angle

Reference framework for Stage 4 (`prompts/04-bq-prep.md`). The substance of a story doesn't change by company — the emphasis and wording do. Use this to angle the Top 20 questions and STAR templates toward the language a specific company's interviewers actually listen for.

## Amazon — Leadership Principles (the most structured; align explicitly)

Amazon's behavioral questions map almost one-to-one to a Leadership Principle. Consciously hit the relevant LP's keywords:

| LP | Meaning | Where to steer the story |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Obsession | Reasoning backward from the customer | Emphasize trade-offs made for the user |
| Ownership | Long-term thinking, never "not my job" | Emphasize seeing it through to the end |
| Dive Deep | Getting into the data details | Emphasize finding a root cause others missed |
| Bias for Action | Speed, reversible decisions made fast | Emphasize decisiveness and small, fast steps |
| Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit | Willing to push back, then fully executes once decided | First choice for conflict stories |
| Deliver Results | Hitting key metrics on time | Emphasize the quantified outcome |
| Invent and Simplify | Innovating and reducing complexity | Emphasize what was simplified |

Amazon interviewers weight **data** and **first-person accountability** heavily. Have a hard number ready for every story, and speak in "I," not "we."

## Meta — impact and speed

- Keywords: **impact, move fast, ownership, scrappy, cross-functional**.
- What they're listening for: quantified impact (users/metrics), progress made under ambiguity, getting things done across teams, self-direction.
- Angle: emphasize **measurable impact** and **speed of execution** — less process talk, more outcome talk.

## Anthropic — mission, rigor, collaboration

- Keywords: **mission-driven, thoughtfulness, safety mindset, honesty, collaboration**.
- What they're listening for: honest reflection (including on failures, without spin), consideration of downstream impact and risk, making colleagues better, clear communication.
- Angle: show **deliberate, considered judgment** and genuine collaboration — not lone-hero framing.

## OpenAI — ambition, agency, shipping under ambiguity

- Keywords: **ambition, agency, ship, high-velocity, first-principles**.
- What they're listening for: defining the problem yourself when no one hands you one, first-principles reasoning, shipping something substantial fast.
- Angle: emphasize **self-initiation** and **delivering something real under uncertainty**.

## General / other companies

- Clear structure (STAR), quantified results, clear personal contribution, and genuine reflection travel well almost everywhere, even without a distinctive company culture to angle toward.
- If the target company isn't in this list, ask the user what they know about the company's stated values (careers page, glassdoor themes, interviewer LinkedIn posts) and angle accordingly — don't assume a generic big-tech culture without a signal for it.
